5. One interesting thing I found from Google’s SEO Starter Guide is the emphasis on creating content for users first, not search engines. 
Google explains that pages should provide clear, useful, and original information that directly answers what users are searching for. 
Instead of stuffing keywords unnaturally into a page like we might think, Google actually recommends writing content that reads naturally 
and organizes information using headings, paragraphs, and descriptive titles. This way, it helps search engines better understand the page 
while also improving the experience for human visitors at the same time, which ultimately leads to better rankings.
